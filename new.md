

**Suggestions that fit this collection's style** — physics, spring animation, Apple-flavored, unusual:

**Input / Form**

- **Morphing Search** — a search pill that expands into a full input with results, morphing border radius as it opens
- **Haptic Toggle** — a switch that physically overshoots and snaps, with a squish animation on the thumb

**Navigation / Layout**
- **Magnetic Dock** — macOS-style icon dock where items bulge toward the cursor with a spring field
- **Elastic Tabs** — tab indicator that stretches horizontally as it travels between tabs, squishing on arrival
- **Stacked Cards Nav** — navigation where pages are stacked like physical cards and you peel them away

**Feedback / Display**
- **Ripple Button** — click produces a physics-accurate pressure wave, not the standard CSS ripple
- **Toast Stack** — toasts that physically stack and fan when multiple arrive, collapse back on hover
- **Particle Cursor** — cursor that emits small particles that fade and fall with gravity

**Data / Utility**
- **Morphing Number** — numeric display where digits scroll and flip like an odometer when the value changes
- **Skeleton Shimmer** — shimmer that follows your mouse position like a specular highlight across skeleton loaders
- **Drag to Reorder List** — list items you can drag with spring-resistance, snapping into new positions with physics

**Creative**
- **Noise Card** — card with a live Perlin noise background that shifts on hover
- **Spotlight Card** — card with a radial gradient spotlight that tracks the cursor across the surface


The ones that would fit most naturally given what's already here: **Magnetic Dock**, **Elastic Tabs**, **Morphing Number**, **Spotlight Card**, and **Haptic Toggle** — they all share the same spring-physics + single-prop-to-customize pattern the existing components follow.
